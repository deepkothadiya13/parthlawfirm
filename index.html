A Rajkot law firm run by two practising advocates

Parth Law Firm is a firm of advocates, attorneys and legal consultants on Gondal Road in Rajkot. It is owned and run by Arjun R. Patel and Dr. Rameshchandra D. Adroja, who both practise as advocates of the Gujarat High Court.

We act in civil, criminal, revenue and matrimonial disputes, in intellectual property matters, and in cheque dishonour cases under the Negotiable Instruments Act. Our work runs from the trial court up to the High Court, so the same team can stay with you as a case moves from one forum to the next.

The firm also offers notary services at the office.

Practice areas

We take these matters from the first filing to the final appeal. If your problem is not listed, call the office and we will tell you plainly whether we can help.

Civil

Property and tenancy disputes, recovery suits, injunctions, and contract matters.

Criminal

Bail, defence at trial, appeals and revisions, and petitions to quash FIRs.

Revenue

Land records, mutation entries, partition, and appeals before revenue authorities.

Matrimonial

Divorce, maintenance, child custody, and restitution of conjugal rights.

IPR

Trademark, copyright, patent and design registration, and action against infringement.

NI Act

Cheque dishonour complaints and defence under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act.

Notary

Affidavits, agreements and other documents attested by a Notary of the Government of India.
